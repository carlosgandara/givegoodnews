# Goodie image preferences

## Communication preferences

- Reply with facts only.
- Do not offer apologies or emotional validation.

## Image preferences

- Avoid props or metaphors that resemble weapons or threats, including pointed thorny sticks or ropes held like knives. For resentment, use an empty clenched hand opening; do not use a thorny object. When a requested metaphor creates a threatening appearance, convey it through facial expression, posture, or harmless abstract imagery instead.
- Do not include subliminal or hidden messages suggesting harmful actions. Avoid unintended imagery resembling suicide, self-harm, strangulation, hanging, restraint, or violence. In particular, never place cables, cords, ropes, straps, or loops around a person's neck or throat. Charging cables must remain on desks and devices, clear of human bodies. Do not wrap or bind people in cables. Convey work pressure through crowded equipment, notifications, posture, and facial expression instead. Check poses, props, shadows, and background symbols for harmful unintended interpretations before accepting an image.
- Do not include rainbows or rainbow motifs, including in background art, puzzles, clothing, props, or decorations.
- This prohibition includes tiny rainbow symbols, multicolor striped or arched emblems, envelope inserts in Goodie's mailbag, postcards, badges, and background details. Reference images do not override this rule. Use plain cream envelopes with no decorative symbols in the mailbag. Inspect every generated image at detail level before accepting it.
- Give adult male characters traditionally masculine appearance, practical clothing, grounded posture, and natural gestures. Do not infer sexuality from appearance.
- Avoid romantic framing or embraces between male characters unless explicitly requested.
- Preserve the established wide three-panel image format, cream borders, givegoodnews.com footer, and original red Goodie mascot. The domain must be exactly givegoodnews.com.
- Goodie delivers the postcard in the middle panel and appears laughing in the final panel.
- Number new images using the highest imageN.png number in img plus one.
- Give each story a distinct emotional conflict and a distinct concrete choice; do not recycle the same solution with new characters.
- Before proposing or creating a new story, read image-story-index.json and compare the problem, Goodie action, principle, and ending with existing entries. Update that index after accepting a new or revised image. Flag close repetitions rather than silently reusing a concept.
- Do not resize images or apply lossy compression unless the user explicitly requests it. Domain-only edits must preserve comic artwork pixels and original dimensions.
